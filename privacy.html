Privacy Policy

The Logic Notebook for Android · effective 11 November 2026

The Logic Notebook is a puzzle app. It works offline and does not collect any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not send data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (puzzles solved, solving times, the puzzle you are working on) and your settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them. If you use Android's backup, your phone may include this progress in your own Google backup, under Google's control and your device settings.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google Play only tells the app whether your Google account owns Pro, so it can unlock the packs. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase: policies.google.com/privacy.

Permissions

The app has no internet permission. The only permission it uses is Google Play's billing permission, which lets the Google Play Store app handle the in-app purchase.

Children

The app is made for adults. Because it collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
